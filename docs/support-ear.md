# Support EAR

## Financially

We rely on financial support from mission-aligned organizations to sustain the EAR community's work.

You can donate to EAR via [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/ecosystemaction). To discuss funding a specific intiative or day-to-day operations, contact the EAR team by [email](mailto:ecosystemaction@proton.me).

The [financial agreement](agreements/financial.md) describes how funds are allocated and used.
