# Opportunities

## Opportunities are everywhere

Just because we can do something doesn't mean we should. A key function of EAR is bringing people together to discuss opportunities and transform shared understanding of potential opportunities based on real-world problems through multiple stages of discovery and conversation.

## Explore an opportunity

Each action research cycle begins with conversation to identify a preliminary diagnosis, gather data, and plan further inquiry. The following inputs are considered during exploration: 

* **Landscape and motivation:** What are the challenges and problems identified?  
* **Participant stakeholders:** Who is affected? What are their roles,  needs, desires, challenges, pain points, and hopes for the future?  
* **Opportunity:** What are desirable outcomes of this inquiry?  
* **Data sources:** What data or research exists today that may be relevant?  
* **Subject matter experts:** What expertise or community of practice may add value to the line of inquiry? Who can be invited?  
* **Scope:** Describe a scenario and various situations to focus the effort.  
* **Assumptions, risks, constraints:** What are the potential risks and mitigating factors?  
* **Activities, deliverables and timeline:** Identify key milestones, touch points, and deliverables. Time box the effort. 

You are welcome to start an opportunity summary to kick off an exploration process for a specific opportunity area. We recommend that you schedule a kick off call either with a workshop format to surface the above, or to review an existing draft. Seeking advice and participation from EAR members is a good way to incorporate multiple perspectives early.
