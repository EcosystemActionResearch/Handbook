## Internal document release process

Every internal and external document version must include a change log and list of contributors.

* Change log  
* Contributors

### Guiding policy

* Two or more people ought to own responsibility for a document.  
  * Accountable (primary)  
  * Responsible (secondary, etc.)  
* While everyone’s feedback is welcome, our goal isn’t that we need every single person involved to give a go or no-go. What might a number of people to determine a stop vs a go may be important  
  * Traffic lights: green yellow red  
  * Provide deadline at least \[X\] business days to review/approve object, share/boost in a Zulip channel (see decisions agreement)  
* Those accountable and responsible should seek feedback.  
* Everyone can give feedback, authoritative votes falls to whom (plural) is responsible  
* How do we ratify that this is done and official (see decisions agreement)

#### Storing final versions of policy documents

* Publishing workflow  
* Track in a versioned environment  
* We want to be able to track change history, who’s involved/attribution

### Informal docs

All EAR members are encouraged to create and circulate internal documents, RFCs, etc.

## External document release process

### Editorial

Material for external release (separate process)

* External EAR comms need to be reviewed and approved by the EAR steering group and project committee.

## Out of scope

### Internal iteration

* Anyone can propose new things within the group.  
* Suggestion: Draft a quick proposal outlining the problem, opportunity, audience voice/tone and purpose/rough outline of why a policy document may provide value. Circulate it for informal review. If you’d like, reach out to a colleague(s) for private review before sharing in a public channel.
