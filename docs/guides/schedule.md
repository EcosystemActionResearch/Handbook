# Schedule a meeting or event

Meetings and events drive our work at EAR. Anyone can schedule an internal meeting. Events must be cleared with convening with notice according to the [decisions agreement](../agreements/decisions/index.md).

For help scheduling a meeting or event, you may consult the [convening service](../agreements/internal-services/convening.md).


Relevent agreement: [Meetings & events](../agreements/meetings-events.md)
