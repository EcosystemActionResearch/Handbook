## Steering functions 

Steering maintains focus on outside and future awareness, and near-term strategy. Steering works closely with both the project committee which is responsible for long-term strategy, and operations services which support day-to-day activities internally.

1. Programs  
   1.1 Upcoming program review, feedback, go/no-go  
   1.2 Potential program preplanning/development and proposals  
2. Policy and communications  
   2.1 Policy  
   2.2 Communications  
3. Horizon scanning: What other challenges or opportunities are on the horizon?  
   3.1 Within EAR  
   3.2 Across ecosystems we work with (starting with AT Protocol)  
4. Highlights & Next steps  
   4.1 Reflecting on and summarizing emergent concerns  
   4.2 Communicating progress and upcoming opportunities to the EAR community and strategic comms working group for external comms.

## Participate in steering

All EAR members with interest in shaping near-term strategy are invited and encouraged to contribute to steering conversations and decisions. To participate in Steering, join the next bi-weekly [Steering Meeting Sync in Luma](https://luma.com/ecosystemaction).
