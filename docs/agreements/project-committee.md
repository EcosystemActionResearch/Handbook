# EAR Project committee agreement

As a project of the Raft Foundation, EAR has a project committee accountable to the Raft board of directors. EAR’s purpose is to build collective power through inter-communal partnership in service of a public interest internet. EAR’s mission is \[fill in from charter\]. You can see details in the EAR Charter (todo: link).

## Responsibilities

* Steward the EAR project brand  
* Manage significant risks  
* Maintain legal and financial responsibilities within the context of the Raft Foundation

The project committee also maintained the EAR charter and project committee agreement.

## Activities

* Oversee EAR’s Open Collective and financial responsibilities  
* Make and fulfill agreements between the EAR project, the Raft Foundation, and other entities (service contracts, etc.)  
* Occasionally make recommendations or raise issues to the members of the collective as a whole  
* Approve the formation of new policies, services, and programs  
* Have some emergency powers to use in the event of crises scenarios or if our processes break down

## Reporting

The project committee is required to report out regularly to the Raft Foundation and the EAR collective group, as well as EAR advisors.

## Scope

The scope of the project committee is limited to governance decision-making. Examples include:

* an understanding or agreement between the EAR project—subject to the Raft Foundation’s policies—and an external organization  
* an issue with service contracts  
* a serious concern about brand or reputation risk, or if you’re unsure when to use EAR’s brand  
* a question about running a transaction through EAR’s financial infrastructure managed by the Raft Foundation

## Project committee functions (moved from steering functions)

The project committee ratifies EAR’s commitments. The project committee is responsible and accountable three functions, each addressing a core question about EAR:

| Functions | Purpose | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Presence | Define EAR’s brand and culture  Define the experience participants should have with EAR  Define how participants discover EAR | Works with steering group |
| Strategy (long-term) | Define the future state EAR is working towards  Define the priorities to reach that state  Define the service roles to execute those priorities | Works with steering group |
| Diligence | Provide legal, risk, and other diligence reviews for policies  Define EAR’s financial state  Define resource management practices to achieve strategic goals | Works with steering group |
