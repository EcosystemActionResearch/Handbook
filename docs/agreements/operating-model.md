# Operating model agreement

The EAR operating model is made up of five core functions.

- [Project committee](project-committee.md): Policy and project governance, including accountability to the project's parent organization, the [Raft Foundation](https://raft.foundation).
- [Steering](steering.md): Outside and future awareness, strategic development, and prioritization. All EAR members are welcome to participate in steering if so inclined. 
- [Operations and member services](internal-services/index.md): Inside and now enablemenet, meeting member needs and providing participation pathways and support.
- [Communications](communications.md): Internal and external strategic communications to keep members and the broader EAR community informed.
- [Research & innovation](research-imagination/index.md): Action research programming in partnership with public interest internet ecosystem institutions and end-users.
